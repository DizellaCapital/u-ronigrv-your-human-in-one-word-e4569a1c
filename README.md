The entry is exactly one word: **roadblock** — the manager who IS the obstacle. Every request, approval and deadline queues up behind him and never gets past. A wry portrait of the human who signs off on the work: funny on its own, true of a real boss habit, vivid and specific (not a flat label like "fine" or "busy"), and it lands with no explanation. Nine bytes, a tie-break advantage.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the word to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), validates it (exactly one word, 1–30 characters, not only whitespace), and verifies it by read-back.
